# Arbiter Policy Gate

Status: **inactive — PPV Foundation has no escrow or arbitration feature.**

This document prevents arbitration from entering code before its user policy
exists. A future proposal must be approved in plain language before custodial
instructions are merged.

At minimum, that policy must define:

- who may serve as arbiter and prohibited conflicts of interest;
- how the arbiter is selected and accepted by both parties;
- evidence formats and submission deadlines;
- acknowledgement and decision service levels;
- whether silence, incomplete evidence, or fraud indicators change procedure;
- the only permitted outcomes between payer and payee;
- fees, refunds, appeals, recusals, replacements, and downtime handling;
- public disclosures and jurisdiction-specific legal terms;
- data retention and deletion behavior for private evidence.

The on-chain arbiter must never be able to route funds to itself or a third
party. Until the policy and the corresponding terms-binding design are
approved, arbitration remains unavailable.
